The People of 9/11

Thousands of individual stories became part of one of the most significant events in modern history.

People Behind the History

Historical events are not only made up of dates and locations. They are also made up of people. On September 11, 2001, thousands of people were directly affected by the attacks. Among them were office workers, travelers, firefighters, police officers, emergency medical workers and ordinary citizens.

Each person experienced that day differently. Some people were inside the World Trade Center, some were working in emergency services, and others were passengers or crew members aboard the four hijacked aircraft.

People and Groups

Firefighters and First Responders

Those Who Ran Toward the Danger

Firefighters, police officers and emergency medical workers responded to the attacks while many people were attempting to leave the affected areas.

Their work became an important part of the historical memory of September 11. Many responders helped evacuate people, treated the injured and searched the affected buildings.

The emergency response involved organizations from New York City as well as agencies from surrounding areas. Their efforts became closely associated with the stories of courage and public service remembered after the attacks.

The Passengers and Crew

Flight 93

United Airlines Flight 93 was one of the four hijacked airplanes. After learning more about what was happening, passengers and crew members attempted to regain control of the aircraft.

The plane eventually crashed in Pennsylvania. The actions of the passengers and crew became an important part of the historical story of September 11.
